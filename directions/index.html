Eight directions for the new Le Jour

Four identities for the new shop, built on what you liked at Venezianico (flow, structure, brand presentation) and on how Christopher Ward presents its range. Same watches, your logo, real photography from lejourwatches.ch. Part one stays close to your references. Part two adds four directions with more character: sunny, lively, memorable. Each has a full homepage and a product page to click through, on desktop and phone.

What we took from your two references

  1. The watch opens the page.

    Venezianico starts with one watch, full width, one line and one button. Every direction here opens the same way.

  2. Story and shop take turns.

    Brand chapter, collections, statement, journal, then the watches. The rhythm keeps people scrolling.

  3. Collections are shown on the wrist.

    Venezianico sells each line through a lifestyle picture and its name. Le Jour has these photos for every line.

  4. The product page tells a story after the price.

    Thumbnails and accordions above, then dark image bands, the movement and a statement below.

  5. Christopher Ward makes a large range easy.

    Families with their own cards, size, movement and water resistance on every card, filters, and delivery promises next to the price.
