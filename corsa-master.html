25 · Questions

Asked often.

How do I use the tachymeter?+

Start the chronograph as you pass a fixed point. Stop it exactly one kilometre later. The number on the bezel next to the seconds hand is your average speed in km/h.

Will it fit my wrist?+

The case is 42 mm wide, 51 mm from lug to lug and 17 mm high. For a smaller wrist, look at the Rally Monte-Carlo at 40 mm.

What does delivered duty paid mean?+

The price includes Swiss VAT and import costs. Swiss Post delivers it, and nothing is due at the door.

Who services my watch?+

Our service partner in Switzerland. The Sellita SW500 is a widely used calibre, so parts and expertise are easy to find for decades.

Can I return it?+

Yes. Within 30 days, unworn, with a free return label to a Swiss address.

What does the warranty cover?+

Five years on the movement and workmanship, from the day of purchase. It transfers to the next owner.
