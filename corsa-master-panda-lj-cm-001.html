05 · The idea

A panda for the road.

In 1980 the Le Jour 7203 was the chronograph insiders bought. The Corsa Master carries that idea forward. A black tachymeter bezel. Black counters on vintage off-white, the contrast that made the 1970s readable at speed. A day-date window, so you know which day you are timing. Forty-two millimetres of steel on a bracelet that closes like it means it.

10 · Numbers you can feel

Three numbers, one watch.

42 MM

Wide enough to read at a glance. 51 mm lug to lug.

200 M

Screw-down crown. The lake, the sea and everything in between.

SW500

Automatic chronograph, 28'800 beats an hour.

15 · Read the dial

Ten seconds to understand a tachymeter.

Press start as you pass a kilometre marker, stop at the next. The bezel tells you how fast you were going.

Keyboard: space to start and stop, R to reset

35 · Questions

Before you decide.

How do I use the tachymeter?+

Start the chronograph as you pass a fixed point. Stop it exactly one kilometre later. The number on the bezel next to the seconds hand is your average speed in km/h.

Will it fit my wrist?+

The case is 42 mm wide, 51 mm from lug to lug and 17 mm high. For a smaller wrist, look at the Rally Monte-Carlo at 40 mm.

What does delivered duty paid mean?+

The price includes Swiss VAT and import costs. Swiss Post delivers it, and nothing is due at the door.

Who services my watch?+

Our service partner in Switzerland. The Sellita SW500 is a widely used calibre, so parts and expertise are easy to find for decades.

Can I return it?+

Yes. Within 30 days, unworn, with a free return label to a Swiss address.

What does the warranty cover?+

Five years on the movement and workmanship, from the day of purchase. It transfers to the next owner.
